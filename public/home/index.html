Before you press record.

What if I forget to mention something?

Spoken only uses what you said. Anything missing stays blank instead of being made up, and you can add it later with a text.

How do I change my site later?

Text the Spoken chat on WhatsApp with your six-character edit code, for example "We open at 8 on Saturdays. Code ••••••". The page updates straight away.

Do you keep my recording?

Your recording is used once, to turn your voice into text. Your site shows only the business details you chose to share.

Where do customer messages go?

Every site has a contact form. Messages are saved for you, and you can read them on your owner page or ask the chat for new ones.

Need bookings, payments or a full app?

Leave your details and what you need. We'll pass your page to a Base44 builder who can grow it into an app.
