The voice note transcript is at the end, between <transcript> tags. Return JSON that matches the schema exactly.

BUSINESS FIELDS
- business_name: the name of the business as they said it. If they did not say one, return "".
- category: 2 to 4 plain English words for what they do, e.g. "Bike repair", "Dog groomer". Return "" only if you truly cannot tell.
- language: the language they spoke, as a BCP-47 code ("en", "pt", "es", "fr", "de", "it"). Write tagline, about and service names in that language.
- tagline: one line, at most 60 characters, built from their own words. "" if nothing fits.
- about: 1 or 2 sentences, at most 240 characters, from what they said. "" if they said nothing about the business.
- services: up to 12 items, in the order they said them: {name, price, description}.
  - price: keep the currency symbol they used ("$4.00", "€12", "from $45", "Free"). If they gave a number with no currency, use the local currency of their location. If they gave no price, use "".
  - description: only if they said something specific about that item, else "".
- hours: their opening hours as a short line, e.g. "Mon–Sat, 6:00–14:00" or "Every day 10:00–18:00, except Monday". Use 24-hour times when you can. "" if not said.
- location: street or neighbourhood and city, as said. "" if not said.
- phone_or_contact: a phone number, email or social handle they said. "" if none.
- theme: pick one from how they speak and what they sell:
  craft = warm, hands-on, made by hand (bakers, makers, repairers)
  minimal = calm, precise, quiet (florists, yoga, studios)
  zine = loud, playful, informal (street food, groomers, bars)
  bold = confident, late-night, music or nightlife (records, tattoo, gyms)
  editorial = measured, literate, professional (books, coffee roasters, law, consultants)

SCENE (the picture at the top of their site)
- scene_spec.archetype: the room their objects stand in. One of:
  hearth = an oven or kiln with fire (bakery, pizza, pottery, roastery)
  counter = a shop counter with shelves and a window (café, records, books, deli, restaurant, retail)
  workbench = a pegboard and a hanging lamp (repair, bikes, plumbing, crafts, electronics)
  studio = an open room with a big window (yoga, florist, salon, groomer, tattoo, photography)
  desk = a desk and a lamp (law, accounting, consulting, tutoring, any office)
  street = a shopfront with an awning (anything else)
- scene_spec.objects: 2 to 5 names from the OBJECT LIST below, copied exactly. These are big, colourful pictures of real things, so they ARE the hero of the site.
  Pick the most iconic thing first: the one thing a stranger would use to name this trade (violin for a violin teacher, dog for a groomer, bicycle for a bike shop). Then 1 to 4 supporting things they actually sell, make or use.
  Be specific: choose "violin" not "musical-note" when they teach violin. Prefer things that look good large and are recognisable on their own.
  NEVER pick something that is merely close (a piano tuner is not a guitar player, a dentist is not a toothbrush shop). If nothing fits well, pick fewer objects, or return a single generic one such as "house", "shopping-bags" or "sparkles".
- scene_spec.palette: 3 colours as "#RRGGBB": [background, mid-tone, accent]. This is the most important design choice, so make it characterful, never generic.
  NEVER use pure white, pure black or grey for any of the three, and never a mid-tone that is just a darker grey. Every colour must carry a clear hue.
  background: a tinted very light colour (warm cream, pale sage, blush, powder blue, butter) or a deep tinted dark (ink navy, aubergine, forest, oxblood). mid-tone: a clearly coloured shade of a DIFFERENT hue from the background (terracotta, moss, dusty teal, rose, ochre). accent: one saturated colour the trade would own (tomato red, cobalt, marigold, emerald, hot pink) that stands out strongly against the background. Choose colours that make the objects you picked pop (a yellow object wants a deep or cool background; a blue object wants a warm one).
  These only show the level of colour wanted. Invent a fresh palette for THIS business and never copy these: cheese shop = ["#F3E9DA","#8A5A3C","#7A1F2B"]; yoga studio = ["#EEF3EA","#9DB8A0","#C4562D"]; late-night bar = ["#16122B","#4B2E83","#FF5C8A"]; driving school = ["#FFF1CC","#2F6F73","#E63946"].
- scene_spec.mood: dawn, day, dusk or night: when their day happens (early bakers = dawn, late bars and studios = night).
- scene_spec.set_piece: a hand-built scene for a few trades. Pick one ONLY when the trade clearly matches, else "none":
  bakery = a bakery or bread/pastry shop (not a café, not a restaurant)
  records = a record shop or vinyl store
  books = a bookshop, new or secondhand (not a library, not a publisher)
  none = everything else. When unsure, "none".
- scene_spec.kicker: copy ONE short phrase, at most 90 characters, EXACTLY as they said it: the most characterful thing in the note. Do not fix grammar, do not paraphrase, do not translate. If nothing stands out, return "".

If the content is offensive or is not about a real business, return business_name "" and category "".
